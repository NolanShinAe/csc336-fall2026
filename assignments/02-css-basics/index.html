The Sims 4

The Sims 4 is a sandbox-esque life simulation game that allows you to create and customize characters called, "Sims," build and furnish incredible homes, and play with life. Whether you choose to control your Sims every action and tell a story or let your Sims roam and choose their own destinies and stories, it is all up to you, the Sim God. The Sims is a very customizable game, whether you like to simulate life or play out fantasy, there are many DLCs you can buy and add to your game. The Sims 4 DLCs are divided into a four categories:

  • Expansion Packs
  • Game Packs
  • Stuff Packs
  • Kits
The most important to know, is that the the Sims 4 free to play. DLCs are not necessary to enjoy the game. As long as you have a mind, anything is possible. Moreover, the Sims 4 has robust modding community. Many of the DLC features can be replicated with just a few mods at cost to you other than keeping them updated when the game gets its monthly bug fixes.
